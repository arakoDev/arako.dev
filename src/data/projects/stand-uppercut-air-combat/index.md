## Core mechanics

The system extends stand combat from grounded attacks into a complete aerial combat sequence:

- **M1 punches:** Delivers close-range basic attacks that can lead into the uppercut and continue during air combat.
- **Uppercut:** Launches the target into the air and creates an opportunity to begin an aerial follow-up.
- **Double jump:** When activated near a recently launched target, positions both characters in the air and starts the air-combat state.
- **Aerial M1 combo:** Allows the attacker to land up to four M1 punches during air combat, with the final hit becoming a downslam that drives the target aggressively into the ground and breaks the floor on impact.
- **Skill follow-up:** Instead of continuing the aerial M1 combo, the attacker can activate a skill, which ends the air-combat state after being used.
