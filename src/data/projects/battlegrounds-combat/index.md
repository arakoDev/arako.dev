## Core mechanics

The system implements the foundational mechanics commonly found in Roblox battlegrounds games:

- **M1 chain:** A sequence of close-range basic attacks that forms the base of combat and combo routes.
- **Uppercut:** A launching attack that sends the opponent upward for aerial follow-ups.
- **Downslam:** An airborne finisher that drives the opponent back into the ground.
- **Front dash:** A forward burst used to close distance, initiate pressure, and connect attacks.
